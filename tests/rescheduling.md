# Rescheduling

Run these conversations with real OpenAI and Cal.com. Book the original meeting through the test account, retain its UID, and find destination slots in the live schedule. Verify that the preview leaves the original intact. Reply “Confirm” to commit a move and read the agenda again. [Shared test setup](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#test-setup)

| Case | Conversation and setup | Pass criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 · Move my meeting later | Book a future opening with at least one later opening on the same local date. Ask “Move my [time] to later today” when the booking is today, or name its actual date. Pick one of the later times shown. | Query live slots with the original `eventTypeId` and `bookingUidToReschedule`. Reschedule the original UID once. A fresh agenda shows one active meeting at the chosen time with the same attendee and duration. Record which date phrasing was exercised. |
| R2 · Several possible originals | “Move my intro with Maya to Friday.” Two Maya intros match. The user identifies Thursday’s intro and chooses an opening. | Resolve the original before writing. The reschedule path contains Thursday’s UID. The other intro keeps its original time. |
| R3 · Destination becomes unavailable | After the bot displays a destination slot, book it for a competing test attendee through Cal.com. Then select that slot in chat. | Explain that the move could not be made, refresh openings, and offer another choice. The agent sends no separate cancel or create request. Read back both the original and competing bookings at their respective times. |
| R4 · Follow-up uses the current UID | Move a real booking, capture the UID Cal.com returns, and say “Actually, cancel it because we already met.” | Cancel the UID from the move response once. Verify the moved meeting is cancelled and unrelated bookings remain active. Record whether Cal.com issued a new UID so the changed-UID regression is only credited when observed. [Cal.com issue 29986](https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy/issues/29986) |
| R5 · Pending host approval | Use a test event type with host confirmation enabled. Create a pending meeting and move it as the attendee. Observe the live response status. | For a pending result, show the new time and say it awaits approval. Record an unexercised pending case if the account configuration auto-accepts the move. [Reschedule status rules](https://cal.com/docs/api-reference/v2/bookings/reschedule-a-booking) |
| Tuesday conflict and Wednesday correction | Put Hellen on Monday at 2:30–3:00 PM and Maya on Tuesday at 2:00–3:00 PM. Ask to move the 2:30 meeting to Tuesday at the same time, then say “then move to wensday”. | The Tuesday reply names Maya and her time. The next reply previews Hellen’s Monday and Wednesday times. Confirming sends one reschedule request for Hellen. Maya stays on Tuesday. |

## Live conversations for moving several meetings

[Executable rescheduling cases](test_multiple_meetings.py) cover R6–R8 and the partial-failure variant. Use an isolated future week. Book Maya and Hellen through the test event with separate Mailosaur aliases. Keep an unrelated Friday control booking and record the originals’ IDs, times and durations. For each preview, verify the originals are still active. After a confirmed move, check the new time and record the ID returned by Cal.com.

| Case | What the user says, in turn order | What should happen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R6 · Move both to the same day | With Maya booked Monday 9:30–10 AM and Hellen Monday 2–3 PM, ask “Can you move both my Monday meetings to Wednesday? Same times as before.” → “Yes, move them both.” | With both Wednesday slots open, show Maya’s Monday 9:30 AM → Wednesday 9:30 AM and Hellen’s Monday 2 PM → Wednesday 2 PM. After approval, the fresh agenda shows both on Wednesday with their original attendees and lengths. The control booking keeps its time. |
| R7 · One destination is blocked | With the R6 originals, book Leo on Wednesday 2–2:30 PM. “Move Maya and Hellen to Wednesday, same times.” → “Okay, just move Maya. Leave Hellen on Monday.” → “Confirm.” | Identify Leo’s overlap immediately. Keep both originals in place while discussing options. The revised preview covers only Maya. After confirmation, Maya is on Wednesday at 9:30 AM, Hellen is on Monday at 2 PM, and Leo is on Wednesday at 2 PM. |
| R8 · Move the same meeting twice across turns | With Maya booked Monday at 10 AM and a separate Maya booking Friday at 10 AM, say “Move my Monday 10 with Maya to Wednesday at the same time.” → “Confirm.” → “Actually, Thursday works better. Move the one we just changed, same time.” → “Confirm.” | Show a fresh preview for each move. The second must start from the active Wednesday booking, using the ID returned by the first move. Finish with Maya on Thursday and Friday. A fresh agenda shows no active Monday or Wednesday copy. |

For a partial-failure variant of R6, start with both destinations open and have a competing test attendee take Hellen’s Wednesday slot just after Maya’s move succeeds. Expect separate outcomes. Maya has moved, Hellen’s original is still active, and proposing another time for Hellen must leave Maya alone.
